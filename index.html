03 / Streaming & Lighting

Bring the
service closer.

Help people follow services online and in the room through cameras, lighting and projection.

  • Frame & followOperate cameras, support live switching and help check the stream.
  • Shape the scenePrepare lighting looks, follow cues and help the room and cameras see clearly.
  • Cue & displayPrepare and display song lyrics, Bible passages, sermon slides and announcements, following the flow of the service.
